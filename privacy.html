Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We collect only what we need to serve you well, and we never sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect the details needed to fulfil it: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and order details. When you join our mailing list, we collect your email address. Like most websites, we also collect basic technical data (such as browser type and pages visited) to understand how the site is used.

How We Use It

We use your information to process and deliver orders, provide customer service, send order updates, and — only if you subscribed — share news about collections and offers. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in every email.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers essential to running the store — such as payment processing and delivery companies — and only to the extent needed for them to do their job. We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Security & Retention

Payment is processed through secure, industry-standard providers; we do not store your full card details. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your Rights

You may ask to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at contact@ninaetmichelle.com.
