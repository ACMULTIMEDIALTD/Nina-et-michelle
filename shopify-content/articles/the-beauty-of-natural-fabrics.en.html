Every collection begins with a thoughtful choice of fabric.

We carefully select materials for their texture, weight, and the way they naturally move with the body. Rather than following trends, we focus on timeless fabrics that feel effortless today and remain beautiful for years to come.

We believe true luxury is found in simplicity — in the softness of natural fibers, refined finishes, and pieces designed to become part of everyday life.

Each fabric is chosen with intention, ensuring every garment offers comfort, longevity, and understated elegance.

At Nina et Michelle, quality is never an extra. It is where every piece begins.
