Shipping

Complimentary shipping on all orders over ₪500. Shipping fees for orders below ₪500 are displayed at checkout.

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of purchase. Once your order has shipped, you will receive a confirmation with tracking details by email.

Delivery takes up to 7 business days from the date of your order.

Self pickup: you may also collect your order free of charge from our studio in Rehovot. Choose “Pickup” at checkout; your order is ready within 2–4 business days, and we will let you know by email as soon as it is ready.

Delivery times refer to business days (Sunday–Thursday, excluding holidays) and may vary slightly during sale periods or new collection launches.

If your order has not arrived within the expected time frame, contact us at contact@ninaetmichelle.com and we will track it down for you.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love every piece. If something isn’t right, you may return or exchange it within 14 days of receiving your order.

To be eligible for a return, items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with all tags attached. Items returned damaged, altered, or without tags cannot be accepted.

To start a return or exchange, email us at contact@ninaetmichelle.com with your order number, and we will guide you through the process.

Return shipping: the cost of the return shipment is deducted from your refund.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 business days of the returned item arriving at our studio and passing inspection. You will receive a confirmation by email once the refund has been processed.

Size exchanges are subject to availability, and cost only the shipping fee. If the item you want is out of stock, we will offer a refund or store credit instead.

Items purchased on final sale are not eligible for return or exchange, unless faulty. If you have received a faulty or incorrect item, contact us and we will make it right at no cost to you.
