Where do you ship?

We currently ship throughout Israel. Shipping options and estimated delivery times are displayed at checkout.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Orders are carefully prepared and dispatched as soon as possible. Delivery takes up to 7 business days. Delays may occasionally occur due to circumstances beyond our control.

Do you offer complimentary shipping?

Complimentary shipping is available on all orders over ₪500. Shipping fees for orders below ₪500 will be displayed at checkout.

Can I cancel my order?

If you wish to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible at contact@ninaetmichelle.com with your order number. If your order has already been processed or dispatched, the cancellation will be handled in accordance with our Returns & Exchanges Policy and applicable law.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. Eligible items may be returned or exchanged in accordance with our Returns & Exchanges Policy and applicable law. Items should be unworn, unused, unwashed and in their original condition with all original tags attached.

How do I request a return or exchange?

Please contact us at contact@ninaetmichelle.com with your order number and the item you wish to return or exchange. We’ll provide you with the relevant instructions.

When will I receive my refund?

Once an eligible return has been received and processed, any refund due will be issued in accordance with our Returns & Exchanges Policy and applicable law. Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

What if I receive a damaged or incorrect item?

Please contact us as soon as possible at contact@ninaetmichelle.com, including your order number and photographs of the item where relevant. We’ll review the issue and assist you accordingly.

How do I choose the right size?

Please refer to the size guide available on each product page. If you’re between sizes or need additional help, feel free to contact us.

Will sold-out pieces be restocked?

Our collections are produced in limited quantities, so restocks are not always guaranteed. Selected pieces may occasionally return.

How should I care for my pieces?

To preserve the quality, fit and fabric of your pieces, we recommend washing at 30°C on a delicate cycle. Do not tumble dry. Always follow any additional care instructions provided on the garment label.

Where are your pieces made?

Each NINA ET MICHELLE piece is thoughtfully designed and carefully produced with close attention to fabric, fit and detail.

Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

Please contact us as soon as possible. If your order has not yet been processed or dispatched, we’ll do our best to update the delivery details. Changes cannot be guaranteed once an order has entered the shipping process.

How can I contact you?

For questions regarding orders, sizing, shipping or our pieces, please contact us at contact@ninaetmichelle.com, or on WhatsApp at 050-699-5056.
