We believe everyone deserves an effortless shopping experience — including visitors with disabilities.

Our Commitment

Nina et Michelle is committed to making its website accessible to people with disabilities, in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities regulations and the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA (Israeli Standard 5568).

What We Have Implemented

The site is built with a clear semantic structure for screen readers; all images carry alternative text; buttons, forms and menus are labelled; color contrast meets the required ratios; the site is fully usable by keyboard; and the layout adapts to any screen size and to browser text-size settings.

Known Limitations

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. If you encounter any difficulty using the site, we would sincerely like to know so we can fix it.

Accessibility Contact

For any accessibility issue, request or suggestion, contact our accessibility coordinator at contact@ninaetmichelle.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
