Our Story

We don’t design for the season.
We design for the years in between.

The Fine Art of Basics

Nina et Michelle was born from a simple idea: that the pieces you wear every day deserve the most care, not the least.

From the first sketch to the final stitch, every detail is considered. We take our time selecting fabrics, perfecting silhouettes, and creating garments that feel effortless to wear.

We don’t believe in overdesign. We believe in thoughtful construction, timeless proportions, and quality that speaks without words.

Materials & Care

Every collection begins with a thoughtful choice of fabric. We select materials for their texture, weight, and the way they naturally move with the body.

True luxury is found in simplicity — in the softness of natural fibers, refined finishes, and pieces designed to become part of everyday life. Cared for gently, they only get better with time.
